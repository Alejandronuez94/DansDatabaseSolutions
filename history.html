History Timeline

May 7th, 1987 - Initial Opening

Our humble origins begin inside a garage shared by a few fresh graduates from the New York City College of Technology (which at the time it was the New York Technical School). Combining their talent and love for computing and networking technologies to form Helping Hand Inc.


May 27th, 1987 - First contract secured

After weeks of searching and advertising their services, Helping Hand Inc. finally secures a contract to outfit the now defunct PAL Learning Center with DIX (short for Digital Equipment Corporation, Intel, and Xerox) standard ethernet.


October 30th, 1987 - Long-Term contract with the PAL Learning Center

Seeing the previous great work provided by Helping Hand Inc. a long-term contract is offered to reorganize the overflowing database of student records.


November 20th, 1994 - Name Change

Helping Hand Inc. is renamed to Dan's Database Solutions to honor the retiring of founding memeber Bob Hasseloffen.


July 18th, 2003 - Acquiring the Dans Database Solutions domain

In the aftermath of the dot-com bubble, the domain for Dans Database Solutions domain is accquired. Up until that point we relyed solely on word of mouth and radio advertisements aired on 101.7 FM.
